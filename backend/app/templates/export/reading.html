Reading the documents

Every article-shaped page was read one at a time and asked the questions a source is asked: who writes it, who it quotes, who it only writes about, where its events happen, when they happened, what it asserts, and what it is for. This page is what {{ A.documents }} such readings add up to. The individual readings are in readings.json; each one names the page it came from.

What came back

Every other page in this archive studies what the publication said. This one studies what readers did with it. Comments survive in the captured markup, so they were read out of the page directly — no AI was involved, nothing here is an estimate, and anyone can verify a line by opening the capture it cites. Because comments accumulate after publication while the article text does not, they were taken from the last surviving capture of each page.
